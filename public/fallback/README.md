# AF-18 static views — Vector PNI 7.1

Actual captures of the procedural AF-18 reconstruction, prepared with Chrome / Playwright during the 9 November 2026 task. They are not photographs, AI images or manufacturer CAD. Source: local 7.1 based on stable commit 551e62cf51c1bc5da35f0c9391bfef4e39d93850.

Overview: 1600 × 1000, 55112 bytes, opaque #edf0f2 background. Camera position (5.8, 6.2, 11.5), look target (0, 0.1, 0), FOV 34°, original 1800 × 1200 renderer cropped 100 px on every edge. Camera and normalized landmark coordinates are preserved from 7.0.

Parts: 15 real exterior previews at 600 × 480; no invented illustration for the external charger. Blue FC solder mask references the official H743-SLIM-V4 example, not a confirmed installed AF-18 revision. Internal construction remains conditional.

WebGL is needed only to prepare captures during development. Production fallback uses ordinary img files, shared component data, 16 cards, 52 textual sections and retry controls independently of a running renderer.
